The Beginning

Lovro Bedenk

Before the mask, there's Lovro. Six years of music school on oboe and piano, two years in an orchestra and over 30 solo performances built a classical foundation long before the first synth came in.

He now studies audio engineering and sound design, refining both the technical and the creative side of sound. On the record he's the calm half: warm synths, orchestral textures and room to breathe.

The End

Blurfnes

Then the mask goes on. Blurfnes is the project Lovro started in 2023, and the alter ego that runs it: heavier, louder, more confrontational. Orchestral textures crash into hard synth basses, raw analog synthesis and bass rooted in trap, with techno cuts like No Time To Waste and Mainstage Suspension along the way.

In 2024 Blurfnes founded Hyard Records and collaborates often with Rave Saint and Blueexnetic. Live sets run on a looping setup inspired by Paul Kalkbrenner: real-time composition, slow build-ups and a fully immersive show.
